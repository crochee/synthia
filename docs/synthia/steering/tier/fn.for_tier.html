Build a tier-tuned steering bundle.

The exact preset for each tier lives in synthia_provider::TierLimits::for_tier. The facade translates those numbers into a concrete guard / hint / tracker combination:

  • the ToolBudgetGuard cap scales with tool_budget,
  • the LoopDetectionGuard window scales with loop_detection_window,
  • the IterationReminderHint cadence is loop_detection_window * 5 (so a small model is reminded every 15 iterations, a large one every 25),
  • the AdaptiveTracker concurrency is max_concurrency,
  • the ContextBudgetHint fires at context_budget_threshold.
